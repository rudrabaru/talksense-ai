# PowerPoint Template Analysis
# TalkSense AI Presentation

---

# Purpose

This document reverse-engineers the official Sarvajanik University presentation template.

All future presentation slides **must strictly follow** this design language.

The objective is **not** to redesign the presentation, but to adapt TalkSense AI content naturally into the existing academic template.

# 2. Branding Guidelines

The presentation follows the official branding of:

- Sarvajanik University
- Sarvajanik College of Engineering and Technology
- Department of Information Technology / Artificial Intelligence & Data Science

Institutional identity is emphasized through:

- University logos
- Department heading
- Consistent color accents
- Standardized title formatting

---

## Logos

### Title Slide

Contains:

- University logo (top-left)
- Institutional emblem (top-right)

### Content Slides

Only the institutional emblem appears in the upper-right corner.

Logos remain fixed across slides.

They should **never be resized, removed, recolored, or repositioned.**

# 6. Title Placement

The title is positioned:

Upper-left

Immediately after the red accent block.

The green divider line begins immediately after the title.

The title should remain on a single line whenever possible.

---

# 7. Content Placement

Bullets begin directly below the title.

Content is aligned to the left.

The remaining slide area is intentionally left open for:

- diagrams
- screenshots
- flowcharts
- architecture figures
- tables

Avoid filling the entire slide with text.

---

# 8. Grid System

The template effectively follows a simple two-zone layout.

Zone 1

Upper heading area

Approximately 15–20% of slide height.

---

Zone 2

Content area

Approximately 80–85% of slide height.

No complex grid system exists.

Layouts should remain visually balanced.

---

# 9. White Space

White space is a defining characteristic.

Rules:

- Large margins
- Wide spacing between bullets
- Generous empty areas
- No crowded layouts

Slides should never appear text-heavy.

---

# 10. Margins

Maintain:

Large top margin

Moderate left margin

Wide right margin

Large bottom margin

Do not place objects too close to slide edges.

---

# 11. Alignment Rules

Use consistent left alignment.

Titles:

Left aligned

Bullets:

Left aligned

Images:

Centered or right aligned

Architecture diagrams:

Centered

Tables:

Centered

Charts:

Centered

Avoid mixed alignments.

---

# 12. Content Density Guidelines

## Recommended Bullet Count

Ideal:

3–4 bullets

Maximum:

5 bullets

Never exceed six bullets.

---

## Bullet Length

Ideal:

8–15 words

Maximum:

18 words

Avoid long paragraphs.

---

## Text Density

Low

The template expects the presenter to explain verbally.

Slides should act as visual support rather than documentation.

---

# 13. Image Placement

Preferred location:

Right half

or

Lower half

of the slide.

Recommended image sizes:

Large enough to be readable.

Avoid multiple unrelated images.

Prefer one dominant visual.

- Accent colors
- Divider line

---

# 23. Slide Consistency Rules

Every slide must:

Use identical title positioning.

Maintain identical spacing.

Use identical font hierarchy.

Contain one primary visual.

Use concise bullets.

Maintain institutional branding.

Avoid visual clutter.

Preserve the academic tone.

---

# 24. Content Adaptation Strategy

The template is intentionally simple.

Instead of increasing text density:

- Convert information into diagrams.
- Replace paragraphs with visuals.
- Use screenshots wherever appropriate.
- Merge related topics into a single slide.
- Keep bullets concise.

The presenter should explain details verbally.

---

# 25. Best Practices for TalkSense AI

For this project, the following visuals are recommended:

- Overall System Architecture
- Audio Processing Pipeline
- AI Workflow
- Technology Stack Diagram
- Dashboard Screenshot
- Live Transcript Screenshot
- Analytics Dashboard
- One-Month Timeline
- Future Scope Roadmap

Avoid creating slides containing only text.

Every technical slide should contain one dominant visual that complements the concise bullet points.

---

# 26. Final Design Principles

The final presentation should feel like an official university presentation rather than a corporate pitch deck.

Guiding principles:

- Academic
- Professional
- Minimal
- Clean
- Technically accurate
- Visually balanced
- Easy to present
- Diagram-oriented
- Consistent across all slides

When generating future slides, always prioritize template consistency over adding excessive information.
